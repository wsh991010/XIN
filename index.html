PROJECTS

What's in the mirror?

From the perspective of issues, I transformed the narrative methods in movies and novels into design elements, and added the appearance of the site to create a narrative atmosphere and experience of the space. We who live in the mirror all our lives, is there any chance to escape?

BO BO BUBBLE

We try to change the shape after inhalation by adding other substances. Add other substances to replace the deformation of the air and control the shape after inhalation. The possibility of all permutations and combinations of interconnecting elements of different numbers is investigated in both rod and edge ways.

Hollowed-out ARC

This is a Cultural&Creative Center. I used arched and semicircular elements to create this semi-open outdoor square plus indoor exhibition workshop. Make use of the local historical context of the base to analyze the design of transportation, terrain and other factors.

Square & Streets

Analyze historic plaza design and use it in the design of collective housing in Taipei City. Analyze the relationship between entrances and exits and aisles of residents.Through the architectural design of residential form, we create a meeting place for residents and tourists.

Subconscious

This is a series of architectural design concept works. Inspired by photographic works, the photos associate space and transform from a flat surface to a three-dimensional conceptual work. Interpret the corresponding relationship between each photo and the environment from a psychological perspective.
